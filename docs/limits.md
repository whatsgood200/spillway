# Limits, stated

What <Product> is not, yet.

- **Network.** <Testnet only, test funds with no value. Or: mainnet with a trivial real amount, unaudited.>
- **Trust.** <Who holds which key and what they could do with it.>
- **Data.** <Where each number comes from, how often, and what it misses.>
- **Simulated parts.** <Anything that runs in a simulator, a mock or a single node, and what the production path would be.>
- **Liquidity and usage.** <Our own accounts and bots, named with their addresses. Outside users: count, or none.>
- **Scope.** <What is designed but not built.>
- **Security.** Not audited. <What testing was done: unit, fuzz, invariant, fork, review.>
- **Dependencies.** <What stops working if a sponsor service or our own server is down.>
- **Legal.** <Anything not an offer of a financial product, and what was not reviewed by counsel.>
